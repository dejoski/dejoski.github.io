Your account

An account is optional. It keeps your notes, saved verses, highlights and reading progress safe and in step across your phone and computer.

Checking your session…

What an account syncs

  • Highlights — the verses you colour in the reader
  • Settings — theme, font, size, translation and the other reading options
  • Learn progress — flashcards, streaks and alphabet practice
  • Where you left off — the chapter “Continue reading” opens
  • Notes — your notes on verses
  • Saved verses — bookmarks and their collections
  • Reading plans — which plans you started and which days are done
  • Reading streak — the days you open a chapter, counted as a run of days in a row

Without an account, everything above except notes and saved verses still works and stays in this browser. When you sign in, what is on this device is merged with your account, so nothing is lost on either side. Privacy: what is stored
