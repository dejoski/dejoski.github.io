Abraham and Lot: uncle, nephew and the parting of ways

FamilyStated in the text

Lot, son of Abraham's dead brother Haran, travelled with Abraham from Haran to Canaan. Their herds outgrew the land and their herdsmen quarrelled, so they separated: Lot chose the Jordan plain near Sodom, Abraham stayed in Canaan. Abraham later rescued Lot from captivity and interceded for Sodom.

Sources: Gen 11:27 · Gen 13:5-12 · Gen 14:12-16 · Gen 18:22-33

Lot and his daughters: the origin of Moab and Ammon

FamilyStated in the text

After Sodom's destruction Lot's daughters bore Moab and Ben-ammi, ancestors of the Moabites and Ammonites, peoples who stay entangled with Israel (Ruth the Moabite is David's great-grandmother). Deuteronomy ties their later status to the Abrahamic kinship of Lot.

Melchizedek and Abraham

RoleDebated

After the rescue of Lot, Melchizedek, king of Salem and priest of God Most High, blessed Abraham, who gave him a tenth. Psalm 110 makes the order of Melchizedek a model for the Davidic king-priest; Hebrews 7 builds on it. Who Melchizedek was is debated.

Where the text shows it: Gen 14:18-20 — Melchizedek blesses Abraham; Abraham gives a tenthPs 110:4 — "A priest forever after the order of Melchizedek"Heb 7:1-3 — Hebrews' reading of Melchizedek
Ways people read this (3)
  • Historical king of Salem (likely Jerusalem), a Canaanite priest-king who worshipped the one God; Hebrews uses him typologically.
  • Some Jewish traditions (including rabbinic sources) identify him with Shem.
  • Some Christian interpreters see a pre-incarnate appearance of the Son; others read Hebrews 7 as typology rather than identity.

Sources: Gen 14:18-20 · Ps 110:4 · Heb 7:1-3
