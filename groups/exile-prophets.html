Josiah's sons: the last kings of Judah

FamilyStated in the text

Three sons of Josiah reigned after him: Jehoahaz (Shallum), Jehoiakim (Eliakim) and Zedekiah (Mattaniah); Jehoiakim's son Jehoiachin also reigned. Pharaoh Necho and Nebuchadnezzar renamed or installed them as vassals.

Is Jeremiah's father Hilkiah the high priest?

FamilyDebated

Jeremiah is called son of Hilkiah, one of the priests in Anathoth. Some identify this Hilkiah with the high priest who found the law book under Josiah; the text never says so, and Anathoth was the town of Abiathar's exiled line.

JeremiahA prophetHilkiahA High Priest
Where the text shows it: Jer 1:1 — Words of Jeremiah son of Hilkiah, of the priests at Anathoth2 Kgs 22:8 — Hilkiah the high priest finds the law book1 Kgs 2:26 — Abiathar exiled to Anathoth
Ways people read this (3)
  • Some older commentators say no: a high priest would be in Jerusalem, and Zadokite priests did not live at Anathoth.
  • Others accept identity, which would explain Jeremiah's access to Josiah's circle.
  • Most modern commentators see no decisive evidence and treat Hilkiah as a common name.

Sources: Jer 1:1 · 2 Kgs 22:8 · 1 Kgs 2:26

Ezekiel and Daniel in exile

WorkDebated

Both lived in Babylon at the same time, Daniel at court from 605 BC, Ezekiel among the deportees from 597 BC. Ezekiel names "Daniel" alongside Noah and Job as a model of righteousness and of wisdom. Whether that is the same Daniel is debated.

Ways people read this (3)
  • Traditional view: Ezekiel refers to his contemporary, then already famous at court.

Belshazzar and Nebuchadnezzar: "father" and "son"

FamilyWidely accepted

Daniel 5 calls Nebuchadnezzar Belshazzar's "father" and Belshazzar his "son". Babylonian records show Belshazzar as the son of Nabonidus and co-ruler, so "father" is usually read as predecessor or ancestor. Nabonidus's wife may have been related to Nebuchadnezzar.

BelshazzarSon of NebuchadnezzarNebuchadnezzarFather of Belshazzar
Where the text shows it: Dan 5:2 — Vessels taken by "his father Nebuchadnezzar"Dan 5:18 — "The Most High God gave Nebuchadnezzar your father kingship"Dan 5:30 — Belshazzar killed that night
Ways people read this (3)
  • The Nabonidus Chronicle and the Verse Account name Belshazzar as Nabonidus's son and regent.
  • Hebrew and Aramaic use of "father" and "son" for ancestors and successors is well attested.
  • Some conservative readings hold he was a literal grandson through his mother.

Sources: Dan 5:2 · Dan 5:18 · Dan 5:30

Isaiah names Cyrus

RoleDebated

Isaiah 44-45 names Cyrus as the LORD's "shepherd" and "anointed" who will release the exiles. Whether the prophecy was written before Cyrus (about 150 years after Isaiah) or by a later prophet in the exile is a major dispute.

IsaiahA prophetCyrusPersian Emperor
Where the text shows it: Isa 44:28 — "Who says of Cyrus, He is my shepherd"Isa 45:1 — Cyrus called "anointed"Ezra 1:1-2 — Cyrus's decree recalls the LORD's stirring
Ways people read this (3)
  • Traditional view: one Isaiah wrote all sixty-six chapters, including a predictive naming of Cyrus.
  • Critical view: chapters 40-55 come from an anonymous sixth-century prophet (often called Second Isaiah) writing in Cyrus's time.
  • Josephus (Antiquities 11.1) says Cyrus read this prophecy; this is a tradition, not independent proof.

Sources: Isa 44:28 · Isa 45:1 · Ezra 1:1-2
