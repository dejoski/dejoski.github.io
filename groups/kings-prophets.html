Kingmaker: Samuel anoints both Saul and David

RoleStated in the text

Saul and David were each anointed privately by Samuel, Saul at Ramah and David at Bethlehem, and both received the Spirit. Because Saul remained king, David lived for years as the anointed king-designate while Saul still reigned, which frames the whole conflict.

David and Jonathan: covenant friendship

FriendsStated in the text

Jonathan, Saul's heir, loved David "as his own soul", made a covenant, gave him his robe and weapons, and warned him of Saul's plots, knowing David would be king. Their bond outlasts Jonathan's death on Gilboa, as David's lament and his kindness to Jonathan's son show.

RoleStated in the text

When Sennacherib of Assyria threatened Jerusalem in 701 BC, Hezekiah sought Isaiah. Isaiah promised deliverance and the Assyrian army was struck down. Isaiah also announced and then reversed Hezekiah's fatal illness, and warned of Babylon after the envoys' visit.
