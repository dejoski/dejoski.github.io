Moses, Aaron and Miriam: a Levite sibling trio

FamilyStated in the text

The children of Amram and Jochebed, of the clan of Kohath in the tribe of Levi. Aaron was the elder brother; Miriam the sister (probably the one who watched the baby Moses in the Nile). Miriam is called prophetess; Aaron became the first high priest.

Jethro and Moses: father-in-law and adviser

FamilyStated in the text

Moses fled to Midian, married Jethro's daughter Zipporah, and tended his flock. After the Exodus Jethro visited, offered sacrifices, and advised Moses to delegate judging to capable men, the basis of Israel's tiered judiciary. He is called Reuel and Jethro, and Moses' brother-in-law Hobab is also named.

Caleb and Joshua: the two faithful spies

FriendsStated in the text

Caleb and Othniel: uncle, nephew, son-in-law

FamilyStated in the text

Caleb offered his daughter Achsah to whoever took Kiriath-sepher; Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother, did so and married her. Othniel later became Israel's first judge. The Kenizzite descent of Caleb shows that non-Israelite clans were absorbed into Judah.

Korah and Moses: cousins in rebellion

FamilyStated in the text

Korah son of Izhar and Moses and Aaron sons of Amram were first cousins, both grandsons of Kohath. Korah challenged Moses and Aaron's exclusive leadership together with Dathan and Abiram of Reuben; the ground swallowed the rebels. Korah's sons did not die, and the "sons of Korah" later appear as temple singers.
