Who are the brothers of Jesus?

FamilyDebated

Mark names James, Joses, Simon, and Jude as brothers of Jesus. Protestants usually hold they were younger half-siblings, Catholic and Orthodox traditions (Hieronymian and Epiphanian views) hold they were cousins or Joseph's sons from an earlier marriage.

Where the text shows it: Mark 6:3 — Brothers namedMatthew 13:55 — Same listGalatians 1:19 — James the Lord's brotherActs 1:14 — Brothers with the disciples
Ways people read this (3)
  • Half-brothers, sons of Joseph and Mary (Helvidius; most Protestants).
  • Step-brothers, sons of Joseph from an earlier marriage (Epiphanius; Eastern tradition).
  • Cousins (Jerome).

James the Lord's brother and James son of Alphaeus

RoleDebated

Paul calls James the Lord's brother an "apostle" in Galatians 1:19. Jerome argued he was the same as James son of Alphaeus, making Jesus' "brothers" cousins. Most other scholars treat them as different men.

JamesSon of JosephJamesAn apostle
Where the text shows it: Galatians 1:19 — Other apostle, James the Lord's brotherMark 3:18 — James son of AlphaeusMark 15:40 — James the younger
Ways people read this (3)
  • Identified (Jerome): one James, cousin of Jesus.
  • Distinct: Lord's brother was not among the Twelve.
  • Paul's "apostle" may mean wider sense.

Philip the apostle or Philip the evangelist?

RoleDebated

Some early writers merged the apostle Philip and the evangelist Philip, placing the daughters in Hierapolis. Acts clearly separates the Twelve from the Seven.
