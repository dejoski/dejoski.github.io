Tell Hum is a ruin near the Sea of Galilee, about two miles south-west of where the river Jordan enters the sea. Khan Minyeh is a Saracen inn on the northern extremity of the plain of Gennesaret (el-Gkuweir), about 5 miles south-west of the mouth of the Jordan and 2 1/2 to 3 miles below Tell Hum, and situated near the lake.
