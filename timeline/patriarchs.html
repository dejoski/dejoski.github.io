  1. 2100 BC – 2091 BC
    Terah's family leaves Ur for Haran
    Genesis 11:27–32· narrative

    c. 2100 BC – Terah's family moves from Ur to Haran, shortly before Abram's call (traditional dating) Best estimate

    Haran dies in Ur, and Abram marries Sarai, who is barren. Terah takes Abram, Lot, and Sarai toward Canaan but settles in Haran, where he dies.

  2. 2100 BC – 1000 BC
    Abraham's other descendants and Edom
    1 Chronicles 1:28–54· genealogy

    From Abraham (c. 2100 BC) through Ishmael, Esau and Edom's chiefs and kings, before Israel's monarchy Best estimate

    Abraham's sons Isaac and Ishmael are named, then Ishmael's sons and Keturah's sons, before Isaac's sons Esau and Israel. The chapter then lists Esau's descendants, the early kings of Edom and the chiefs of Edom.

  3. 2100 BC – 1700 BC
    Job's blameless life and large family
    Job 1:1–5· narrative

    Prologue: undated patriarchal-age setting in Uz (c. 2100–1700 BC, traditional); Job's flocks mark his wealth Best estimate

    Job is introduced as an upright, very wealthy man of Uz with ten children. He offers sacrifices after his children's feasts in case they have sinned.

  4. 2100 BC – 1700 BC
    Satan challenges Job before the LORD
    Job 1:6–12· dialogue

    Prologue scene in heaven; earthly setting is the undated patriarchal age in Uz (c. 2100–1700 BC) Best estimate

    In a scene in heaven, the LORD points to Job as a model of faithfulness. Satan argues that Job serves God only because he is blessed, and the LORD permits him to take Job's possessions but not harm Job himself.

  5. 2100 BC – 1700 BC

    Job 1:13–19· narrative

    Prologue: disasters strike Job in undated patriarchal-age Uz (c. 2100–1700 BC, traditional) Best estimate

    On the day his children feast together, four messengers arrive in quick succession. Raiders and lightning take his livestock and servants, and a windstorm kills all his children.

  6. 2100 BC – 1700 BC

    Job 1:20–22· narrative

    Prologue: undated patriarchal-age setting in Uz (c. 2100–1700 BC, traditional); Job's flocks mark his wealth Best estimate

  7. 2100 BC – 1700 BC

    Prologue: undated patriarchal-age setting in Uz; second heavenly test of Job Best estimate

    The heavenly court meets again, and the LORD notes that Job has kept his integrity. Satan says Job would curse God if his own body were struck, and the LORD allows this but forbids taking his life.

  8. 2100 BC – 1700 BC

    Job 2:7–10· narrative

    Prologue: undated patriarchal-age setting in Uz; second heavenly test of Job Best estimate

    Job is struck with painful sores and sits among the ashes scraping himself. His wife urges him to curse God and die, but he replies that people should accept adversity as well as good from God.

Events in this era

Big events in the wider world while these passages were happening.

  1. 2004 BC
    The city of Ur falls

    Ur, the great city Abraham's family is said to come from, loses its power — the world of the patriarch stories is one of city kings rising and falling.

  2. 1792 BC – 1750 BC
    Hammurabi rules Babylon and issues his law code

    His famous laws show the customs of the world Abraham's family lived in — many echo the laws and family rules in Genesis.
