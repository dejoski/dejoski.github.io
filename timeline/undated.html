  1. 1700 BC – 586 BC
    The line of Levi and the high priests
    1 Chronicles 6:1–15· genealogy

    Levi's line to the high priests, down to Jehozadak, exiled to Babylon in 586 BC Best estimate

    The sons of Levi are named, and the priestly line is traced from Aaron through Eleazar and Phinehas down to Jehozadak, who went into exile under Nebuchadnezzar. Moses and Miriam are noted as Amram's children.

  2. Undated
    Judah's clans and the prayer of Jabez
    1 Chronicles 4:1–10· genealogy

    Judah's clans and Jabez – no date stated; covers many generations of settlement Best estimate

    Various clans of Judah are listed, and the list pauses to describe Jabez, said to be more honorable than his brothers. His mother named him for pain, and he prayed for blessing and God granted it.

  3. Undated
    More Judahite families and craftsmen
    1 Chronicles 4:11–23· genealogy

    Judahite families and craftsmen – no date stated; covers many generations Best estimate

    Further families of Judah are listed, including Othniel, Caleb son of Jephunneh and the descendants of Shelah. Notes mention craftsmen, linen workers and royal potters.

  4. Undated
    Benjamin's families and Shaharaim
    1 Chronicles 8:1–13· genealogy

    Benjamin's families and Shaharaim – no dates; spans many generations Best estimate

    Benjamin's sons and grandsons are listed, followed by Shaharaim, who had sons in Moab. Descendants of Elpaal are noted as builders of Ono and Lod and as people who drove out inhabitants of Gath.

  5. Undated

    1 Chronicles 8:14–28· genealogy

    Lists of sons of Beriah, Elpaal, Shimei, Shashak and Jeroham are given. All are called heads of families who lived in Jerusalem.

  6. Undated
    Humans can mine hidden treasures

  8. Undated
