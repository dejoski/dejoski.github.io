Heli was the father of Mary and the maternal grandfather of Jesus.

🗺 Places mentioned alongside Heli on the map →

Heli is mentioned in the genealogy of Jesus Christ in the Gospel of Luke. He is identified as the father of Mary, the mother of Jesus, and thus, the maternal grandfather of Jesus (Luk.3.23). In the genealogy, Luke traces Jesus' lineage back to Adam through his mother Mary's side of the family, while Matthew's genealogy (Matt 1.1-17) follows Joseph's lineage. The mention of Heli provides insight into Jesus' family background and affirms his connection to the Davidic line through his mother. No further information is given about Heli in the Bible. (Luk.3.23).

Joseph's father: Jacob or Heli

FamilyDebated

Matthew says Joseph's father was Jacob; Luke says Joseph was the son of Heli. Africanus reconciled this with a levirate marriage; others read Luke's line as Mary's and TIPNR lists Heli as Mary's father.

Where the text shows it: Matthew 1:16 — Jacob begat Joseph.Luke 3:23 — Joseph the son of Heli.
Ways people read this (3)
  • Levirate: Jacob and Heli were half brothers (Africanus).
  • Mary's genealogy: Luke lists Heli as Mary's father through Joseph.
  • Two different lines of royal and natural descent.

Sources: Matthew 1:16 · Luke 3:23 · Julius Africanus, Letter to Aristides (cited in Eusebius, Church History)
