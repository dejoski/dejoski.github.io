Zadok was a prominent high priest who served during the reigns of King David and King Solomon.

🗺 Places mentioned alongside Zadok on the map →

Zadok was a significant figure in the history of Israel, serving as a high priest during the reigns of King David and King Solomon. He first appears in the biblical narrative as one of the two priests mentioned alongside Ahimelech during David's reign. Zadok remained loyal to David during the rebellion of Absalom, and he played a key role in anointing Solomon as king. After Solomon's accession to the throne, Zadok was appointed as the sole high priest, replacing Abiathar. Zadok's descendants continued to serve as high priests in the temple in Jerusalem, and they are mentioned in the visions of Ezekiel regarding the future restoration of the temple and its services. The "sons of Zadok" are described as faithful priests who will serve in the restored temple. Zadok's lineage and role emphasize the importance of the priesthood in maintaining the worship of God and the spiritual leadership of the nation.

Solomon versus Adonijah: the succession struggle

RoleStated in the text

Adonijah, backed by Joab and Abiathar, declared himself king. Nathan and Bathsheba got David to confirm Solomon, and Zadok, Nathan and Benaiah anointed him at Gihon. Solomon then had Adonijah and Joab killed and sent Abiathar to Anathoth.

Zadok and Abiathar: two priestly lines under David

WorkStated in the text

Abiathar, son of Ahimelech (Eli's line through Ithamar), escaped Saul's massacre of Nob and joined David. Zadok (line of Eleazar) served with him under David. Solomon's removal of Abiathar fulfilled the word against Eli's house and left Zadok's line as the principal priests.

Bible dictionary

Zadok Hastings Hastings’ Dictionary of the Bible (1898)

ZA'DOK (just). Son of Ahitub, of the family of Eleazar, and one of the two high priests in the time of David, Abiathar being the other. 2 Sam 8:17. He joined David at Hebron, 1 Chr 12:28, and was always faithful to him, staying behind in Jerusalem at his request during Absalom's rebellion, and subsequently anointed Solomon king, 1 Kgs 1:39, and was rewarded by Solomon for his faithful service by being made sole high priest. See Abiathar. A priest in the time of King Ahaziah. 1 Chr 6:12. The father of King Uzziah's wife. 2 Kgs 15:33; 2 Chr 27:1. One who repaired a part of the wall. Neh 3:4. 6, 7. Three other persons. Neh 3:29; Gen 10:21; Josh 13:13.
