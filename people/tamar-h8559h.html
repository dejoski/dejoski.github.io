Tamar was the beautiful daughter of King David and full sister of Absalom (2Sa.13.1; 1Ch.3.9). She was raped by her half-brother Amnon, who then rejected her (2Sa.13.1-19). Tamar's tragedy led to Absalom's murder of Amnon and eventual rebellion against David (2Sa.13.20-39). Her story illustrates the dysfunctional relationships and violence within David's family.

Amnon, Tamar and Absalom: rape and revenge

FamilyStated in the text

Amnon, David's firstborn, raped his half-sister Tamar, encouraged by his cousin Jonadab. David was angry but did nothing; Tamar's full brother Absalom killed Amnon two years later and fled. The narrative presents this as the first fulfilment of Nathan's word that the sword would not leave David's house.
