Angels and spiritual beings

  1. The angel meets Hagar at the spring
    Genesis 16:7–14 · dialogue · 2081 BC – 2080 BC

    The angel finds Hagar fleeing and tells her to return and submit. He promises many descendants and says she will bear Ishmael, and she names God 'the God who sees me'.

    Mercy and compassionNames of God
  2. Angels at Lot's house and the mob
    Genesis 19:1–11 · narrative · 2067 BC – 2066 BC

    Two angels arrive at Sodom and Lot insists they stay with him. The men of the city surround the house demanding the visitors, and Lot offers his daughters instead. The angels pull him in and strike the crowd with blindness.

    Sin and the fallPurity and sexual ethics
  3. The angels urge Lot to flee
    Genesis 19:12–22 · narrative · 2067 BC – 2066 BC

    The angels warn Lot to gather his family since the city will be destroyed. His sons-in-law don't take it seriously. The angels lead Lot's family out, and Lot asks to go to nearby Zoar.

    Mercy and compassionSalvation
  4. Angels and news of Esau's approach
    Genesis 32:1–8 · narrative · 1909 BC – 1908 BC

    Angels meet Jacob and he names the place Mahanaim. He sends messengers to Esau, who return saying Esau is coming with four hundred men, and Jacob in fear divides his company in two.

    Providence
  5. The angel and a promise of blessing
    Exodus 23:20–26 · speech · 1446 BC – 1445 BC

    God says he is sending an angel ahead to guard them and lead them to the promised place. If they obey, God will be an enemy to their enemies, bless their food and water, and remove sickness.

    PromiseObedience
  6. The angel and the talking donkey
    Numbers 22:22–35 · miracle · 1407 BC – 1406 BC

    The angel of the LORD blocks Balaam's path three times, seen only by his donkey, who turns aside and is beaten. God opens her mouth to protest, then opens Balaam's eyes. He confesses and is told to go on but to speak only God's words.

    Miracles and signsGod's sovereignty
  7. The Commander of the LORD's army
    Joshua 5:13–15 · dialogue · 1406 BC

    Near Jericho, Joshua meets a man with a drawn sword who says he is the Commander of the LORD's army. He tells Joshua to remove his sandals because the ground is holy.

    HolinessWar and conflict
  8. The angel of the LORD rebukes Israel at Bochim
    Judges 2:1–5 · speech · 1390 BC – 1370 BC

    The angel of the LORD reminds Israel of His promise and command not to make covenants with the land's peoples. Because Israel has disobeyed, those peoples will remain as thorns, and the people weep and offer sacrifices.

    Sin and the fallCovenant
  9. The angel of the LORD calls Gideon
    Judges 6:11–24 · dialogue · 1175 BC – 1160 BC

    The angel of the LORD finds Gideon threshing wheat in a winepress and calls him a mighty man of valor. Gideon protests that his clan is weak, asks for a sign, and sees fire consume his offering, then builds an altar called The LORD Is Peace.

    Election and callingFaith
  10. The angel announces Samson's birth
    Judges 13:1–7 · narrative · 1100 BC – 1080 BC

    Israel is under Philistine rule for forty years when the angel of the LORD tells the childless wife of Manoah of Zorah that she will bear a son. He will be a Nazirite from birth and will begin Israel's deliverance from the Philistines, so she must avoid wine and unclean food.

    Election and callingFamily and marriage
  11. Manoah asks for guidance and the angel returns
    Judges 13:8–14 · dialogue · 1100 BC – 1080 BC

    Manoah prays that the man of God return to teach them how to raise the boy. The angel comes again to the woman in the field, and when Manoah asks, repeats that she must follow the earlier instructions.

    PrayerFamily and marriage
  12. The angel ascends in the altar flame
    Judges 13:15–23 · narrative · 1100 BC – 1080 BC

    Manoah offers to prepare a meal, but the angel tells him to offer a burnt offering instead and says his name is beyond comprehension. As the flame rises the angel ascends in it, Manoah fears they will die, and his wife reasons that the LORD would not have accepted the offering if He meant to kill them.

    Dreams, visions and revelationWorship and praise
  13. The plague and David's plea
    2 Samuel 24:15–17 · narrative · 975 BC – 970 BC

    A plague kills seventy thousand from Dan to Beersheba. When the angel reaches Jerusalem the LORD stops it, and David asks that the punishment fall on him rather than the people.

    Mercy and compassionWrathPrayer
  14. The two cherubim
    1 Kings 6:23–30 · narrative · 966 BC – 959 BC

    Solomon makes two large olive-wood cherubim, overlaid with gold, for the inner sanctuary. Their wings span the room, and the walls and floor are carved and gilded.

    Temple and tabernacleHoliness
  15. An angel feeds Elijah
    1 Kings 19:5–8 · narrative · 870 BC – 855 BC

    An angel twice wakes Elijah and gives him bread and water. Strengthened, he travels forty days and nights to Horeb, the mountain of God.

    Mercy and compassionProvidence
  16. Micaiah's vision of the heavenly council
    1 Kings 22:19–28 · vision · 853 BC – 852 BC

    Micaiah describes seeing the LORD on his throne and a spirit sent to deceive Ahab's prophets. Zedekiah strikes him, and Ahab has him jailed, but Micaiah says Ahab's return will prove him right.

    Dreams, visions and revelationGod's sovereigntyFalse teaching
  17. Elijah's message of death for Ahaziah
    2 Kings 1:3–8 · dialogue · 852 BC

    The angel of the LORD sends Elijah to intercept the messengers and ask why they consult Baal-zebub instead of God. Elijah announces that Ahaziah will die, and the king recognizes the messenger as Elijah from their description.

    IdolatryProphecy
  18. Crossing the Jordan; Elijah taken to heaven
    2 Kings 2:7–12 · miracle · 852 BC

    Elijah parts the Jordan with his cloak, and Elisha asks for a double portion of his spirit. A chariot and horses of fire separate them, Elijah goes up in a whirlwind, and Elisha tears his clothes in grief.

    Miracles and signsThe Holy Spirit
  19. Blinded army led to Samaria and fed
    2 Kings 6:15–23 · miracle · 852 BC – 841 BC

    Elisha's servant sees the hills full of fiery horses and chariots after Elisha prays. Elisha then has the Arameans struck with blindness, leads them to Samaria, and persuades the king to feed them and send them home, ending the raids.

    Miracles and signsPeace and reconciliation
  20. The Assyrian army is struck down
    2 Kings 19:35–37 · miracle · 701 BC

    The angel of the LORD strikes down 185,000 in the Assyrian camp overnight. Sennacherib returns to Nineveh, where his sons kill him while he worships.

    SalvationMiracles and signs
  21. Plague strikes; David pleads for the people
    1 Chronicles 21:14–17 · narrative · 979 BC – 972 BC

    A plague kills seventy thousand, and an angel stretches a sword over Jerusalem before the LORD says 'Enough!' David and the elders fall in sackcloth, and David asks that the punishment fall on him rather than on the people.

    Mercy and compassionWrathPrayer
  22. The cherubim and the veil
    2 Chronicles 3:10–14 · narrative · 966 BC – 959 BC

    Two large gold-covered cherubim with outstretched wings are made for the Most Holy Place. Solomon also makes a veil of blue, purple, and crimson yarn with cherubim woven into it.

    Temple and tabernacleGlory
  23. Micaiah's vision and imprisonment
    2 Chronicles 18:18–27 · prophecy · 853 BC

    Micaiah describes a vision of God's heavenly court in which a spirit volunteers to be a lying spirit in the prophets' mouths. Zedekiah strikes him, and Ahab has him jailed until he returns safely.

    Dreams, visions and revelationGod's sovereignty
  24. Prayer answered; the Assyrians fall
    2 Chronicles 32:20–23 · miracle · 701 BC

    Hezekiah and the prophet Isaiah cry out to heaven, and the LORD sends an angel who wipes out the Assyrian commanders and warriors. Sennacherib goes home in disgrace and is killed by his own sons, while many bring gifts to Jerusalem and Hezekiah is honored.

    SalvationPrayer
  25. Satan challenges Job before the LORD
    Job 1:6–12 · dialogue · 2100 BC – 1700 BC

    In a scene in heaven, the LORD points to Job as a model of faithfulness. Satan argues that Job serves God only because he is blessed, and the LORD permits him to take Job's possessions but not harm Job himself.

    God's sovereigntyTemptation and spiritual warfare
  26. Second heavenly test: Job's health
    Job 2:1–6 · dialogue · 2100 BC – 1700 BC

    The heavenly court meets again, and the LORD notes that Job has kept his integrity. Satan says Job would curse God if his own body were struck, and the LORD allows this but forbids taking his life.

    God's sovereigntySuffering
  27. Ascribe glory to the LORD
    Psalms 29:1–2 · praise · 1010 BC – 970 BC

    The psalmist calls on heavenly beings to honor the LORD's glory and strength and worship Him in holy splendor.

    GloryWorship and praise
  28. I sought the LORD, and he answered
    Psalms 34:4–7 · narrative · 1012 BC – 1010 BC

    He tells how he sought the LORD and was freed from his fears. The poor man called, and the LORD saved him, and God's angel encamps around those who fear Him.

    SalvationFaith
  29. Sentence on the gods and a plea
    Psalms 82:5–8 · oracle of judgment

    The gods are said to lack understanding, though once called sons of the Most High, and are sentenced to die like mortals. The psalmist then prays for God to judge the earth, since the nations belong to him.

    Justice and judgmentGod's sovereignty
  30. Angels guard the one who trusts
    Psalms 91:9–13 · wisdom

    Because the reader has made the Most High their dwelling, no evil will befall them. God commands his angels to guard them, and they will tread safely on lions and serpents.

    FaithHope and endurance
