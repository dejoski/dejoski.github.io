Temple and tabernacle

251 passages · Kingdom

  1. Offering for a sanctuary
    Exodus 25:1–9 · law · 1446 BC

    God asks the Israelites to give freewill offerings of metals, yarn, skins, wood, oil, spices and gems. These materials will build a sanctuary so that God may dwell among them, following a pattern he will show.

    Generosity and giving
  2. The ark and mercy seat
    Exodus 25:10–22 · ritual instruction · 1446 BC

    God gives dimensions for a gold-covered wooden ark with carrying poles, to hold the Testimony, and a gold mercy seat with two cherubim. God says he will meet and speak with Moses there.

    Atonement and sacrificeHoliness
  3. The table for the bread
    Exodus 25:23–30 · ritual instruction · 1446 BC

    God describes a gold-covered table with rings and poles, along with gold plates and bowls. The Bread of the Presence is to be kept on it before God at all times.

    Worship and praise
  4. The golden lampstand
    Exodus 25:31–40 · ritual instruction · 1446 BC

    God details a lampstand of hammered gold with six branches, almond-blossom cups and seven lamps, made from one talent of gold. He reminds Moses to follow the pattern shown on the mountain.

    Worship and praise
  5. Curtains and coverings of the tent
    Exodus 26:1–14 · ritual instruction · 1446 BC

    God specifies ten linen curtains with cherubim, eleven goat-hair curtains for a tent over them, and outer coverings of red ram skins and fine leather. Clasps join them into one unit.

  6. Frames, bases and crossbars
    Exodus 26:15–30 · ritual instruction · 1446 BC

    God gives the design for upright acacia frames set in silver bases, with crossbars held by gold rings, forming the south, north and west walls. Moses is told again to build it as shown on the mountain.

  7. The veil and entrance curtain
    Exodus 26:31–37 · ritual instruction · 1446 BC

    God describes a veil that will divide the Holy Place from the Most Holy Place, where the ark sits, and where to place the table and lampstand. A curtain will also cover the tent entrance.

    Holiness
  8. The bronze altar
    Exodus 27:1–8 · ritual instruction · 1446 BC

    God gives dimensions for a square hollow altar of acacia wood covered in bronze, with horns on the corners, bronze utensils, a grate and carrying poles.

    Atonement and sacrifice
  9. The courtyard enclosure
    Exodus 27:9–19 · ritual instruction · 1446 BC

    God describes a courtyard of linen curtains hung on posts with bronze bases and silver hooks, 100 by 50 cubits, with an embroidered gate curtain. All the tent pegs and utensils are to be bronze.

  10. Oil for the lamps
    Exodus 27:20–21 · law · 1446 BC

    God tells the Israelites to supply pure olive oil so the lamps burn continually. Aaron and his sons will tend them from evening to morning as a lasting statute.

    Priesthood
  11. Daily offerings and God dwelling among them
    Exodus 29:38–46 · ritual instruction · 1446 BC

    God commands two lambs to be offered daily, morning and twilight, with flour, oil and wine. He promises to meet the people there, consecrate the place by his glory and dwell among them as their God who brought them out of Egypt.

    Worship and praisePromise
  12. The altar of incense
    Exodus 30:1–10 · ritual instruction · 1446 BC

    God describes a small gold-covered altar to be placed before the veil, where Aaron will burn incense every morning and twilight. No unauthorized offerings may be made on it, and once a year atonement is made on its horns.

    Worship and praisePrayer
  13. Call for offerings and skilled workers
    Exodus 35:4–19 · speech · 1446 BC

    Moses tells the people what the LORD commands: bring willing offerings of materials and let skilled craftsmen come make everything on the list, from the tabernacle to the priestly garments.

    Generosity and givingWork and rest
  14. Building the curtains and coverings
    Exodus 36:8–19 · narrative · 1446 BC – 1445 BC

    The craftsmen make the ten linen curtains with cherubim, the eleven goat-hair curtains, and the coverings of ram skins and leather. The description repeats the commands given earlier almost word for word.

    Work and rest
  15. Building the frames and crossbars
    Exodus 36:20–34 · narrative · 1446 BC – 1445 BC

    The workers make the upright acacia frames, silver bases, crossbars and gold overlay, forming the walls of the tabernacle exactly as commanded.

    Work and rest
  16. Making the veil and entrance curtain
    Exodus 36:35–38 · narrative · 1446 BC – 1445 BC

    The workers make the cherub-embroidered veil with its gold-covered posts and silver bases, and the entrance curtain with its five posts and bronze bases.

    Obedience
  17. Making the ark and mercy seat
    Exodus 37:1–9 · narrative · 1446 BC – 1445 BC

    Bezalel makes the gold-covered ark with its rings and poles, and the gold mercy seat with two hammered cherubim spreading their wings over it.

    Work and rest
  18. Making the table
    Exodus 37:10–16 · narrative · 1446 BC – 1445 BC

    Bezalel makes the gold-covered table with its rim, rings and poles, and its gold plates, dishes, bowls and pitchers.

    Work and rest
  19. Making the lampstand
    Exodus 37:17–24 · narrative · 1446 BC – 1445 BC

    Bezalel makes the lampstand of one piece of hammered gold with six branches, almond-blossom cups, seven lamps and its tools, using one talent of gold.

    Work and rest
  20. Incense altar, oil and incense
    Exodus 37:25–29 · narrative · 1446 BC – 1445 BC

    Bezalel makes the gold-covered incense altar with its rings and poles, and also the sacred anointing oil and pure incense.

    Work and rest
  21. Making the bronze altar and basin
    Exodus 38:1–8 · narrative · 1446 BC – 1445 BC

    Bezalel makes the bronze-covered altar of burnt offering with its horns, utensils, grate and poles. He also makes the bronze basin and stand from the mirrors of the women who served at the tent entrance.

    Atonement and sacrifice
  22. Making the courtyard
    Exodus 38:9–20 · narrative · 1446 BC – 1445 BC

    The courtyard is made of linen curtains hung on posts with bronze bases and silver hooks, with an embroidered gate curtain, and bronze tent pegs.

  23. The inventory and its makers
    Exodus 38:21–23 · list · 1446 BC – 1445 BC

    The tabernacle's inventory was recorded by the Levites under Ithamar. Bezalel made everything the LORD commanded, with Oholiab as his assistant.

    Obedience
  24. Totals of gold, silver and bronze
    Exodus 38:24–31 · list · 1446 BC – 1445 BC

    The passage records the amounts of gold, silver and bronze used, including silver from the half-shekel payment of 603,550 men. It says what the metals were used for, such as bases, hooks and the bronze altar.

    Atonement and sacrifice
  25. Work finished and inspected
    Exodus 39:32–43 · narrative · 1445 BC

    The Israelites complete all the work and bring every part of the tabernacle and garments to Moses. He inspects it, sees it was done as the LORD commanded, and blesses them.

    ObedienceWork and rest
  26. Instructions for setting up the tabernacle
    Exodus 40:1–15 · ritual instruction · 1445 BC

    The LORD tells Moses to set up the tabernacle on the first day of the first month, place its furnishings, anoint everything, and wash, clothe and anoint Aaron and his sons as priests.

    Priesthood
  27. Moses sets up the tabernacle
    Exodus 40:16–33 · narrative · 1445 BC

    On the first day of the first month of the second year, Moses assembles the tabernacle, places the ark, table, lamps, altars and basin, and hangs the curtains. The text repeats that he did each thing as the LORD commanded.

    Obedience
  28. The glory of the LORD fills the tabernacle
    Exodus 40:34–38 · narrative · 1445 BC

    A cloud covers the tent and the glory of the LORD fills it, so Moses cannot enter. The cloud's lifting or staying guides the Israelites' travels, and fire is in it by night.

    GloryFaithfulness
  29. The LORD introduces offerings to Israel
    Leviticus 1:1–2 · law · 1445 BC

    The LORD calls to Moses from the Tent of Meeting and tells him to instruct the Israelites about bringing animal offerings from the herd or flock.

    Atonement and sacrificeWorship and praise
  30. Keeping Israel from defiling the sanctuary
    Leviticus 15:31–33 · exhortation · 1445 BC

    The Israelites are to be kept apart from their uncleanness so they do not die by defiling the LORD's dwelling among them. A summary then repeats the types of discharge these laws cover.

    HolinessDeath and mortality
