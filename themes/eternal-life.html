Eternal life

40 passages · Salvation

Deuteronomy · 2Psalms · 3Proverbs · 2Ezekiel · 2Daniel · 1Matthew · 2Mark · 1Luke · 3John · 9Romans · 41 Timothy · 12 Timothy · 1Titus · 1Hebrews · 11 John · 3Revelation · 4
  1. Choose life or death
    Deuteronomy 30:15–20 · exhortation · 1406 BC – 1210 BC

    Moses sets before Israel life and prosperity or death and disaster, calling heaven and earth as witnesses. He urges them to choose life by loving the LORD, obeying him, and holding fast to him.

    ObedienceCovenantFaith
  2. Moses urges Israel to take the song to heart
    Deuteronomy 32:44–47 · exhortation · 1406 BC – 1210 BC

    After reciting the song with Joshua, Moses tells the people to take all his words to heart and teach them to their children. These words are their life and will keep them long in the land.

    ObedienceLaw and commandments
  3. You will show me the path of life
    Psalms 16:9–11 · praise · 1010 BC – 970 BC

    The psalmist is glad and confident that God will not leave him in Sheol or let His faithful one see decay. God shows him the path of life and joy in His presence.

    Death and mortalityJoy and thanksgiving
  4. A table prepared and a lifelong home
    Psalms 23:5–6 · praise · 1010 BC – 970 BC

    The image changes to a host who prepares a meal in front of enemies and anoints him. He ends confident that goodness and mercy will follow him and he will live in the LORD's house forever.

    Mercy and compassionFaithfulness
  5. God is my portion forever
    Psalms 73:23–28 · praise

    The psalmist says God holds his hand, guides him and will receive him, so he wants nothing in heaven or earth but God. Those far from God will perish, but it is good for him to draw near.

    FaithWho God isHope and endurance
  6. Blessed are those who listen
    Proverbs 8:32–36 · speech

    Wisdom concludes by urging her hearers to keep her ways: whoever finds her finds life and God's favor, and whoever misses her harms himself.

    WisdomObedience
  7. Wisdom's banquet invitation
    Proverbs 9:1–6 · parable

    Wisdom is pictured as a hostess who builds her house, prepares a feast and sends servants to invite the simple to leave folly and live.

    Wisdom
  8. The righteous man lives
    Ezekiel 18:5–9 · teaching · 592 BC – 591 BC

    God describes a righteous man who worships no idols, treats others justly and mercifully, and lives by God's statutes. Such a person will surely live.

    Justice and judgmentObedienceLoving others
  9. The wicked who turn will live
    Ezekiel 18:21–24 · teaching · 592 BC – 591 BC

    If the wicked turn from their sins and keep God's statutes, they will live and not die, and past sins will be forgotten. God takes no pleasure in the death of the wicked.

    RepentanceMercy and compassionJustice and judgment
  10. Deliverance, resurrection and sealed words
    Daniel 12:1–4 · prophecy · 536 BC – 535 BC

    Michael will arise in a time of unparalleled distress, and those whose names are written in the book will be delivered. Many who sleep in the dust will awake, some to everlasting life and some to shame, and the wise will shine. Daniel is told to seal the book.

    ResurrectionFinal judgmentAngels and spiritual beings
  11. The rich young man
    Matthew 19:16–22 · dialogue · AD 30

    A young man asks what good he must do for eternal life. Jesus points him to the commandments, and when the man says he has kept them, Jesus tells him to sell his possessions and give to the poor, and the man goes away sad because he was very wealthy.

    Wealth and povertyObedience
  12. Reward for leaving everything
    Matthew 19:27–30 · dialogue · AD 30

    Peter says the disciples have left everything and asks what they will get. Jesus promises that they will sit on twelve thrones in the renewal of all things and that all who leave much for him will receive a hundredfold and eternal life, adding that many who are first will be last.

    Discipleship
  13. The rich man's question
    Mark 10:17–22 · dialogue · AD 30 – AD 33

    A man runs up and asks how to inherit eternal life. Jesus points to the commandments, then, looking at him with love, tells him to sell what he has, give to the poor and follow him, and the man leaves sad.

    Wealth and poverty
  14. The good Samaritan
    Luke 10:25–37 · parable · AD 29 – AD 30

    An expert in the law asks how to inherit eternal life, and they agree on loving God and neighbor. Asked who is his neighbor, Jesus tells of a Samaritan who cared for a wounded man ignored by a priest and a Levite, and says to go and do likewise.

    Loving othersMercy and compassionParables
  15. The rich ruler and the cost of following
    Luke 18:18–30 · dialogue · AD 29 – AD 30

    A wealthy ruler asks how to inherit eternal life; Jesus tells him to sell everything and follow. The man leaves sad, and Jesus says entering the kingdom is hard for the rich, but possible with God.

    Wealth and povertyDiscipleship
  16. The Sadducees ask about resurrection
    Luke 20:27–40 · dialogue · AD 30

    Sadducees, who deny resurrection, pose a puzzle about a woman who married seven brothers. Jesus answers that those in the age to come do not marry, and that Moses calls God the God of the living.

    ResurrectionLaw and commandments
  17. God so loved the world
    John 3:16–21 · teaching · AD 27 – AD 28

    Jesus (or John's reflection on His words) says God gave His Son so that believers would have eternal life, that He came to save rather than condemn, and that people avoid the Light because of their deeds.

    LoveSalvation
  18. The One from above
    John 3:31–36 · teaching · AD 27 – AD 29

    Speaking of the One who comes from heaven, the speaker says He testifies to what He has seen, speaks God's words with the Spirit without limit, and those who believe in the Son have eternal life.

    FaithWrath
  19. The Son gives life and judges
    John 5:19–29 · teaching · AD 28 – AD 29

    Jesus says the Son does what He sees the Father doing, gives life and has been given authority to judge. Those who hear and believe pass from death to life, and all will hear His voice at the resurrection.

    Father, Son and SpiritResurrectionFinal judgment
  20. I am the bread of life
    John 6:30–40 · teaching · AD 29

    The crowd asks for a sign like the manna. Jesus says the Father gives true bread from heaven and declares Himself the bread of life, promising that all who come and believe will have eternal life.

    Faith
  21. Eating flesh and drinking blood
    John 6:52–59 · teaching · AD 29

    The Jews argue about how He can give His flesh to eat. Jesus says that eating His flesh and drinking His blood brings eternal life and abiding in Him, unlike the manna.

    Baptism and the Lord's SupperThe cross
  22. I and the Father are one
    John 10:22–30 · dialogue · AD 29 – AD 30

    At the Feast of Dedication in winter, the Jews ask Him to say plainly whether He is the Christ. He points to His works, says His sheep hear His voice and are secure, and declares 'I and the Father are one.'

    Father, Son and SpiritThe Messiah
  23. I am the resurrection and the life
    John 11:17–27 · dialogue · AD 30

    Lazarus has been in the tomb four days. Martha meets Jesus, who says He is the resurrection and the life and asks if she believes. She confesses He is the Christ, the Son of God.

    ResurrectionFaith
  24. Jesus prays for glory
    John 17:1–5 · prayer · AD 30 – AD 33

    Looking to heaven, Jesus tells the Father that the hour has come. He asks to be glorified, says he has finished the work he was given, and defines eternal life as knowing the Father and himself.

    GloryPrayer
  25. Why this Gospel was written
    John 20:30–31 · other · AD 80 – AD 100

    The writer explains that Jesus did many other signs not recorded here. These are written so readers may believe Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and have life in his name.

    FaithMiracles and signs
  26. God repays each one impartially
    Romans 2:6–11 · argument · AD 56 – AD 57

    Paul says God will repay everyone according to their deeds, giving eternal life to those who persevere in doing good and wrath to those who reject truth. This applies to Jew and Greek alike, since God shows no favoritism.

    Justice and judgmentObedience
  27. The gift is greater than the trespass
    Romans 5:15–21 · teaching · AD 56 – AD 57

    Paul contrasts Adam's trespass, which led to condemnation, with Christ's act of obedience, which brings justification and life. Where sin increased, grace increased all the more.

    GraceJustification and righteousness
  28. The wages of sin and gift of God
    Romans 6:20–23 · argument · AD 56 – AD 57

    Paul contrasts the outcome of the old life, shameful and ending in death, with the outcome of serving God, which leads to holiness and eternal life. He sums it up: sin pays wages of death, but God gives eternal life as a gift.

    Sin and the fallDeath and mortalityGrace
  29. Living by the flesh or by the Spirit
    Romans 8:5–11 · teaching · AD 56 – AD 57

    Paul contrasts a mind set on the flesh, which leads to death, with a mind set on the Spirit, which leads to life and peace. The Spirit of the one who raised Jesus lives in believers and will give life to their bodies.

    The Holy SpiritDeath and mortality
  30. Charge to the man of God
    1 Timothy 6:11–16 · exhortation · AD 62 – AD 65

    Paul tells Timothy to flee these evils, pursue righteousness, and fight the good fight. He charges him to keep the commandment faithfully until Christ appears, closing with praise of God's majesty.

    DiscipleshipGod's sovereignty
