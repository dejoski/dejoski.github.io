  1. The LORD alone is God
    Deuteronomy 4:32–40 · speech · 1407 BC – 1406 BC

    Moses asks whether any people has ever heard God speak from fire or been rescued by such signs as Israel was. He concludes that the LORD alone is God and calls them to keep His commands.

  2. Hear, O Israel: love the LORD
    Deuteronomy 6:1–9 · exhortation · 1407 BC – 1406 BC

    Moses explains that these commands are to be kept so Israel may prosper in the land. He declares that the LORD is one, commands love for Him with all one's being, and tells them to keep His words on their hearts, teach their children, and display them.

  3. Why God chose Israel
    Deuteronomy 7:7–11 · teaching · 1407 BC – 1406 BC

    Moses says God chose Israel not because they were numerous but because He loved them and kept His oath. God is faithful to those who love Him and repays those who hate Him, so they should obey.

  4. What God asks of Israel
    Deuteronomy 10:12–22 · exhortation · 1407 BC – 1406 BC

    Moses asks what God requires: to fear Him, walk in His ways, love and serve Him. He says God owns everything, cares for the fatherless, widow, and foreigner, and Israel should love foreigners too. He urges them to circumcise their hearts.

    Deuteronomy 11:1–9 · exhortation · 1407 BC – 1406 BC

    Moses calls Israel to love God and keep His commands, reminding them that they themselves saw the acts of God in Egypt, at the Red Sea, and in the wilderness. Obedience will give them strength to take the land.

    ObedienceExodus and deliverance
