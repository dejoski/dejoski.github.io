Dreams, visions and revelation

  1. Abraham hosts three visitors
    Genesis 18:1–8 · narrative · 2067 BC – 2066 BC

    The Lord appears to Abraham as three men approach his tent. Abraham runs to welcome them and has Sarah and his servant prepare a generous meal.

    Loving othersPromise
  2. God warns Abimelech in a dream
    Genesis 20:1–7 · narrative · 2067 BC – 2066 BC

    Abraham again calls Sarah his sister, and Abimelech takes her. God warns him in a dream that she is married, and Abimelech pleads his innocence, so God tells him to return her.

    God's sovereignty
  3. Jacob's dream at Bethel
    Genesis 28:10–15 · vision · 1929 BC

    On the road to Haran, Jacob sleeps with a stone for a pillow and dreams of a ladder reaching to heaven with angels on it. God stands above and promises him the land, many descendants, blessing to all families, and His presence.

    CovenantPromise
  4. Joseph's two dreams
    Genesis 37:5–11 · narrative · 1898 BC

    Joseph tells his brothers and father two dreams in which sheaves and then the sun, moon, and stars bow to him. The brothers grow more hateful and Jacob rebukes him but keeps the matter in mind.

    Providence
  5. Two royal officials join Joseph in prison
    Genesis 40:1–8 · narrative · 1887 BC – 1886 BC

    Pharaoh's cupbearer and baker are imprisoned and put under Joseph's care. One night both have troubling dreams, and Joseph says that interpretations belong to God and asks to hear them.

    Providence
  6. The cupbearer's dream and its meaning
    Genesis 40:9–15 · dialogue · 1887 BC – 1886 BC

    The cupbearer describes a vine with three branches and Pharaoh's cup. Joseph says it means he will be restored in three days and asks him to remember him to Pharaoh.

    Providence
  7. The baker's dream and the outcomes
    Genesis 40:16–23 · narrative · 1887 BC – 1886 BC

    The baker recounts his dream of three baskets and birds, and Joseph says he will be executed in three days. Both come true on Pharaoh's birthday, but the cupbearer forgets Joseph.

    Death and mortality
  8. Pharaoh's troubling dreams of cows and grain
    Genesis 41:1–8 · narrative · 1885 BC

    Pharaoh dreams of seven thin cows eating seven fat ones, then of thin heads of grain swallowing plump ones. His court's wise men cannot explain either dream.

    Wisdom
  9. Joseph is brought in; Pharaoh tells his dreams
    Genesis 41:14–24 · dialogue · 1885 BC

    Joseph is hurriedly cleaned up and brought to Pharaoh. He credits God, not himself, with any answer, and Pharaoh recounts both dreams.

    Pride and humility
  10. Joseph explains: seven good years, seven famine
    Genesis 41:25–32 · speech · 1885 BC

    Joseph says the two dreams are one message from God: seven years of abundance will be followed by seven years of severe famine. The repeated dream shows God has settled the matter.

    ProvidenceWisdom
  11. God speaks to Jacob at Beersheba
    Genesis 46:1–4 · narrative · 1876 BC – 1875 BC

    On the way to Egypt, Jacob offers sacrifices, and God reassures him in a vision: do not fear, God will make him a great nation there and bring his descendants back.

    PromiseCovenant
  12. Moses meets God at the burning bush
    Exodus 3:1–6 · narrative · 1447 BC – 1446 BC

    While shepherding, Moses sees a bush burning without being consumed. God calls to him from it, tells him to remove his sandals on holy ground, and identifies himself as the God of Moses' ancestors.

    HolinessElection and calling
  13. God comes down on Sinai
    Exodus 19:16–19 · narrative · 1446 BC

    On the third day thunder, lightning, a thick cloud, and a loud ram's horn make the camp tremble. Moses leads the people to the foot of the mountain, which is wrapped in smoke and shakes, and God answers Moses in the thunder.

    GloryHoliness
  14. Leaders see God and share a meal
    Exodus 24:9–11 · narrative · 1446 BC

    Moses, Aaron, his sons and seventy elders go up and see the God of Israel above a pavement like sapphire. God does not harm them, and they eat and drink in his presence.

    GloryCovenant
  15. Moses asks to see God's glory
    Exodus 33:18–23 · dialogue · 1446 BC

    Moses asks to see God's glory. The LORD says he will let his goodness pass by and proclaim his name, but Moses cannot see his face and live, so he will be sheltered in a rock cleft and see God's back.

    GloryMercy and compassion
  16. The LORD speaks from the mercy seat
    Numbers 7:89 · narrative · 1445 BC

    When Moses enters the Tent of Meeting, he hears the LORD speaking from above the mercy seat between the two cherubim.

    Temple and tabernacle
  17. The LORD defends Moses
    Numbers 12:4–9 · speech · 1445 BC

    God summons the three to the Tent of Meeting. He says Moses is unique among prophets, faithful and spoken to face to face, and asks why they did not fear to speak against him.

    ProphecyLeadership
  18. God forbids Balaam to go
    Numbers 22:9–14 · dialogue · 1407 BC – 1406 BC

    God asks Balaam who his visitors are, then tells him not to go and not to curse Israel, because the people are blessed. Balaam sends the princes home.

    ObediencePromise
  19. Secret things and revealed things
    Deuteronomy 29:29 · wisdom · 1406 BC – 1210 BC

    Moses says that hidden matters belong to the LORD, but what he has revealed belongs to Israel and their children so that they may follow the law.

    ObedienceLaw and commandments
  20. Gideon overhears the dream of the barley loaf
    Judges 7:9–15 · narrative · 1175 BC – 1160 BC

    That night the LORD tells Gideon to go down to the camp and, if afraid, listen with his servant Purah. He hears a Midianite describe a dream of a barley loaf toppling a tent, which his friend interprets as Gideon's sword, and Gideon worships.

    Faith
  21. The angel ascends in the altar flame
    Judges 13:15–23 · narrative · 1100 BC – 1080 BC

    Manoah offers to prepare a meal, but the angel tells him to offer a burnt offering instead and says his name is beyond comprehension. As the flame rises the angel ascends in it, Manoah fears they will die, and his wife reasons that the LORD would not have accepted the offering if He meant to kill them.

    Angels and spiritual beingsWorship and praise
  22. The LORD calls Samuel three times
    1 Samuel 3:1–9 · narrative · 1095 BC – 1085 BC

    In a time when God's word was rare, Samuel hears a voice in the night and thinks it is Eli. After the third time, Eli realizes it is the LORD and tells Samuel how to answer.

    Prophecy
  23. Samuel announces Saul's fate
    1 Samuel 28:15–19 · speech · 1011 BC – 1010 BC

    Samuel tells Saul that the LORD has turned away and given the kingdom to David because Saul disobeyed over Amalek. He says Saul and his sons will die and Israel will fall to the Philistines.

    Justice and judgmentObedience
  24. God comes down in storm to rescue him
    2 Samuel 22:8–20 · poetry · 1010 BC – 975 BC

    David describes God's coming with earthquake, fire, dark clouds and thunder to scatter the enemy. He says God reached down and drew him out of deep waters.

    GlorySalvation
  25. David introduces his last words
    2 Samuel 23:1–3 · poetry · 971 BC – 970 BC

    David opens a final poem, claiming the Spirit of the LORD spoke through him. He begins to quote God's saying about how a ruler should govern.

    The Holy SpiritKings and kingship
  26. God offers Solomon anything at Gibeon
    1 Kings 3:5–15 · dialogue · 970 BC – 967 BC

    In a dream God tells Solomon to ask for anything. Solomon asks for an understanding heart to govern, and God grants wisdom and adds riches and honor.

    WisdomPrayer
  27. God appears to Solomon again
    1 Kings 9:1–9 · speech · 946 BC – 945 BC

    God tells Solomon he has heard his prayer and consecrated the temple. He promises Solomon's throne if he stays faithful, but warns that turning to other gods would bring exile and ruin.

    CovenantObedience
  28. God's quiet voice at Horeb
    1 Kings 19:9–14 · dialogue · 870 BC – 855 BC

    God asks what Elijah is doing there, and Elijah says he alone remains faithful. God's presence is not in wind, earthquake, or fire but in a still, small voice, and Elijah repeats the same complaint.

    Who God isSuffering
  29. Micaiah's vision of the heavenly council
    1 Kings 22:19–28 · vision · 853 BC – 852 BC

    Micaiah describes seeing the LORD on his throne and a spirit sent to deceive Ahab's prophets. Zedekiah strikes him, and Ahab has him jailed, but Micaiah says Ahab's return will prove him right.

    God's sovereigntyFalse teachingAngels and spiritual beings
  30. Solomon asks for wisdom
    2 Chronicles 1:7–10 · dialogue · 970 BC – 969 BC

    That night God invites Solomon to ask for anything. Solomon recalls God's kindness to David and asks for wisdom and knowledge to lead such a large people.

    WisdomPrayer
