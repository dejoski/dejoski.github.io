Image of God

8 passages · Humanity

Genesis · 4Deuteronomy · 1Psalms · 3
  1. Humanity made in God's image
    Genesis 1:26–31 · narrative

    God decides to make humankind in his image, male and female, to rule over the animals. He blesses them, gives plants for food, and calls all he has made very good.

    Creation
  2. Creation retold: man from the dust
    Genesis 2:4–7 · narrative

    A second account begins, zooming in on the earth before plants grew. God forms the first man from the ground's dust and breathes life into him.

    Creation
  3. The man names the animals
    Genesis 2:18–20 · narrative

    God observes it is not good for the man to be alone and brings the animals to him to name. Among them, no suitable helper is found for him.

    Family and marriage
  4. The book of Adam's generations
    Genesis 5:1–5 · genealogy

    This section begins with a reminder that God made humankind in his likeness, then records Adam's age at Seth's birth, his later years, and his death at 930.

    Family and marriage
  5. Do not make idols
    Deuteronomy 4:15–24 · exhortation · 1407 BC – 1406 BC

    Since Israel saw no form at Horeb, Moses warns against making an idol of any kind or worshiping the sun, moon, and stars. He reminds them of their rescue from Egypt and says God is a consuming fire, and that he himself will not cross the Jordan.

    Idolatry
  6. What is man that you care for him?
    Psalms 8:3–4 · poetry · 1010 BC – 970 BC

    Looking at the moon and stars, the psalmist wonders why the God who made them cares about human beings.

    Creation
  7. Humans crowned with glory and given rule
    Psalms 8:5–8 · poetry · 1010 BC – 970 BC

    God made humans a little lower than angels, crowned them with glory, and gave them care of animals, birds, and fish.

    CreationGlory
  8. Fearfully and wonderfully made
    Psalms 139:13–18 · praise · 1010 BC – 970 BC

    David praises God for forming him in the womb and for seeing him before he was born. He treasures God's thoughts, more numerous than the sand, and says that when he wakes he is still with God.
