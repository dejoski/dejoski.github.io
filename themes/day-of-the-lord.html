The Day of the Lord

29 passages · Life and end

Isaiah · 4Ezekiel · 1Joel · 3Amos · 3Obadiah · 1Zephaniah · 3Zechariah · 2Malachi · 2Luke · 1Acts · 11 Thessalonians · 12 Thessalonians · 22 Peter · 3Jude · 1Revelation · 1
  1. The proud will be humbled on the Day of the LORD
    Isaiah 2:10–22 · oracle of judgment · 750 BC – 735 BC

    A coming Day of the LORD will bring down everything proud, from cedars and towers to trading ships, and idols will be abandoned. People will hide in caves, so Isaiah urges them to stop trusting in mere humans.

    Pride and humility
  2. The Day of the LORD brings terror
    Isaiah 13:6–16 · oracle of judgment · 720 BC – 700 BC

    The Day of the LORD is pictured as cruel destruction, with people paralyzed by fear, stars dark and the earth shaking. God says He will punish the world's arrogance and its people will flee or be killed.

    Final judgmentWrath
  3. Judgment falls on Edom
    Isaiah 34:5–10 · oracle of judgment · 712 BC – 690 BC

    The LORD's sword comes down on Edom, pictured as a great sacrificial slaughter at Bozrah. It is called a day of vengeance on behalf of Zion, and Edom's land becomes burning tar and sulfur.

    Justice and judgmentWrathThe nations
  4. The warrior returns from Edom
    Isaiah 63:1–6 · vision · 700 BC – 500 BC

    A questioner asks who comes from Edom with garments stained red, and the answer says he is mighty to save. He describes trampling the nations alone as in a winepress, because the day of vengeance had come.

    WrathSalvation
  5. The day of the LORD against Egypt
    Ezekiel 30:1–9 · oracle of judgment · 587 BC – 571 BC

    Ezekiel is told to wail because the Day of the LORD is near, a day of doom for the nations. A sword will fall on Egypt and its allies from Cush to Libya.

    The nationsWrath
  6. The Day of the LORD and the great army
    Joel 2:1–11 · prophecy · 835 BC – 400 BC

    Joel calls for the ram's horn to be blown in Zion because the Day of the LORD is near. He describes an unstoppable army devouring the land like fire, causing heaven and earth to tremble, led by the LORD himself.

    WrathJustice and judgment
  7. The nations judged for harming Judah
    Joel 3:1–8 · oracle of judgment · 835 BC – 400 BC

    The LORD says that when he restores Judah he will gather the nations in the Valley of Jehoshaphat to judge them for scattering Israel and selling people as slaves. Tyre, Sidon and Philistia will have their actions turned back on them.

    The nationsJustice and judgment
  8. The valley of decision
    Joel 3:9–17 · oracle of judgment · 835 BC – 400 BC

    The nations are called to prepare for war, turning plowshares into swords, and to come to the valley for judgment. God is pictured as a harvester and winemaker, while being a refuge for his people in Zion.

    The nationsWar and conflict
  9. The Day of the LORD is darkness
    Amos 5:18–20 · oracle of judgment · 760 BC – 753 BC

    Amos warns those who long for the Day of the LORD that it will be darkness and not light, like fleeing a lion only to meet a bear.

    Final judgment
  10. A basket of summer fruit
    Amos 8:1–3 · vision · 760 BC – 753 BC

    God shows Amos a basket of ripe summer fruit and says the end has come for Israel, with temple songs turning into wailing.

    Dreams, visions and revelation
  11. God will not forget
    Amos 8:7–10 · oracle of judgment · 760 BC – 753 BC

    God swears never to forget their deeds. The land will quake, the sun will set at noon, and feasts will become mourning like mourning for an only son.

    Justice and judgment
  12. The Day of the LORD and Jacob's deliverance
    Obadiah 1:15–18 · prophecy · 586 BC – 550 BC

    The focus widens to the Day of the LORD, when all nations will be repaid as they acted. On Mount Zion there will be deliverance, and Jacob's house will consume Edom like fire burns stubble.

    Justice and judgmentSalvation
  13. The Day of the LORD's sacrifice
    Zephaniah 1:7–13 · oracle of judgment · 640 BC – 622 BC

    The prophet calls for silence because the Day of the LORD is near. God will punish officials, the violent and deceitful, and merchants, and will search Jerusalem for the complacent who think God does nothing, whose wealth will be plundered.

    Justice and judgmentHoliness
  14. The great day of wrath
    Zephaniah 1:14–18 · oracle of judgment · 640 BC – 612 BC

    The Day of the LORD is described as near and terrible: a day of wrath, darkness, and battle cries. Neither silver nor gold can save anyone on it.

    WrathFinal judgment
  15. Seek the LORD before the day comes
    Zephaniah 2:1–3 · exhortation · 640 BC – 612 BC

    Zephaniah urges the shameful nation to gather and turn to the LORD before His anger arrives. The humble who do His justice are told to seek righteousness and humility, in the hope of being sheltered.

    RepentancePride and humility
  16. The nations attack; the LORD stands on Olivet
    Zechariah 14:1–5 · prophecy · 518 BC – 480 BC

    A day of the LORD is coming when nations capture Jerusalem and half the city goes into exile. The LORD goes out to fight them and his feet stand on the Mount of Olives, which splits in two, creating a valley of escape.

    The end timesThe nationsSalvation
  17. Plague and panic on Jerusalem's enemies
    Zechariah 14:12–15 · prophecy · 518 BC – 480 BC

    The LORD will strike the attacking nations with a plague and panic, so they turn on one another. Judah will fight and collect the nations' wealth, and the plague will strike their animals too.

    WrathThe nations
  18. The day of burning and the sun of righteousness
    Malachi 4:1–3 · prophecy · 460 BC – 430 BC

    God says the coming day will burn like a furnace, leaving the arrogant as stubble, while for those who fear His name the sun of righteousness will rise with healing in its wings.

    SalvationHope and endurance
  19. Remember the law and Elijah's return
    Malachi 4:4–6 · exhortation · 460 BC – 430 BC

    God tells them to remember the law of Moses and says He will send Elijah before the great Day of the LORD, turning hearts of fathers and children to each other so He does not strike the land with a curse.

    ProphecyLaw and commandmentsObedience
  20. Days of the Son of Man
    Luke 17:22–37 · teaching · AD 29 – AD 30

    Jesus tells his disciples the Son of Man's coming will be sudden and unmistakable, like lightning. He compares it to the days of Noah and Lot, urges readiness, and, asked where, answers with a proverb about vultures.

    The end timesFinal judgment
  21. Peter explains it through Joel's prophecy
    Acts 2:14–21 · speech · AD 30

    Peter denies that the believers are drunk, since it is only morning. He says this is what the prophet Joel foretold: God pouring out His Spirit on all people, with salvation for everyone who calls on the Lord.

    ProphecyThe Holy SpiritSalvation
  22. The Day of the Lord and being ready
    1 Thessalonians 5:1–11 · teaching · AD 50 – AD 51

    Paul says the Day of the Lord will come like a thief in the night, suddenly for those saying 'Peace and security'. Believers are children of the light and should stay awake and sober, armed with faith, love and hope, since God appointed them for salvation.

    The end timesSalvationHope and endurance
  23. Relief for the afflicted at Christ's return
    2 Thessalonians 1:6–10 · teaching · AD 50 – AD 51

    Paul says God will repay those who afflict believers and give relief to the oppressed when Jesus is revealed from heaven. Those who reject the gospel will face eternal destruction, while Christ will be glorified in His people.

    Justice and judgmentFinal judgmentHope and endurance
  24. Do not be alarmed: the Day has not come
    2 Thessalonians 2:1–5 · teaching · AD 50 – AD 51

    Paul asks them not to be shaken by claims, even ones claiming to be from him, that the Day of the Lord has already come. It will not come until the rebellion and the revealing of the man of lawlessness, who exalts himself as God in the temple.

    The end timesFalse teaching
  25. Scoffers ask where his coming is
    2 Peter 3:1–7 · teaching · AD 64 – AD 68

    Peter says this second letter is a reminder to recall the prophets' words and the apostles' commands. In the last days scoffers will ask where the promised coming is, ignoring that God's word made the earth, judged it with the flood and reserves the present heavens and earth for fire.

    The end timesFalse teaching
  26. The Lord's patience and the Day coming
    2 Peter 3:8–10 · teaching · AD 64 – AD 68

    Peter says a day is like a thousand years to the Lord, and he is not slow but patient, not wanting anyone to perish but all to repent. The Day of the Lord will still come like a thief, with the heavens passing away and the earth laid bare.

    Mercy and compassionRepentanceFinal judgment
  27. Live in holiness and grow in grace
    2 Peter 3:11–18 · exhortation · AD 64 – AD 68

    Since all will be destroyed, Peter asks his readers to live in holiness and look forward to a new heaven and earth where righteousness dwells. He calls them to be at peace, be on guard against error, notes Paul's letters can be hard to understand, and ends with a call to grow in grace and knowledge.

    HolinessGraceHeaven and the new earth
  28. Enoch's prophecy of the Lord's coming
    Jude 1:14–16 · prophecy · AD 60 – AD 80

    Jude quotes a prophecy attributed to Enoch that the Lord will come with thousands of his holy ones to judge the ungodly. He describes the intruders as grumblers who follow lusts and flatter for gain.

    Final judgmentProphecyAngels and spiritual beings
  29. Sixth seal: the day of wrath
    Revelation 6:12–17 · vision · AD 68 – AD 96

    A great earthquake shakes the earth, the sun goes black, the moon turns red, and stars fall. All people hide and call for the mountains to fall on them, since the great day of the wrath of God and the Lamb has come.

    WrathFinal judgment
