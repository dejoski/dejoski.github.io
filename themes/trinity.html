Father, Son and Spirit

28 passages · God

Matthew · 2Mark · 2Luke · 3John · 101 Corinthians · 1Galatians · 1Ephesians · 2Colossians · 1Hebrews · 21 Peter · 11 John · 2Revelation · 1
  1. Jesus is baptized
    Matthew 3:13–17 · narrative · AD 27 – AD 29

    Jesus comes from Galilee to be baptized by John, who hesitates but consents when Jesus says it fits God's purposes. As Jesus comes up from the water, the Spirit descends like a dove and a voice from heaven calls him the beloved Son.

    Baptism and the Lord's SupperObedience
  2. Jesus' prayer and invitation to the weary
    Matthew 11:25–30 · prayer · AD 28 – AD 29

    Jesus thanks the Father for revealing these things to little children, and says only the Son truly knows the Father. He then invites all who are weary and burdened to come to him, take his yoke and find rest.

    Dreams, visions and revelationMercy and compassion
  3. Jesus is baptized and named Son
    Mark 1:9–11 · narrative · AD 27 – AD 29

    Jesus comes from Nazareth and is baptized by John. The Spirit descends like a dove and a voice from heaven calls him the beloved Son.

    Baptism and the Lord's SupperThe Holy Spirit
  4. Transfiguration on the mountain
    Mark 9:1–8 · vision · AD 29 – AD 33

    After promising some will see the kingdom come with power, Jesus takes Peter, James and John up a mountain, where he is transformed in brilliant white with Moses and Elijah. A voice from a cloud calls him the beloved Son and says to listen to him.

    GloryDreams, visions and revelation
  5. Jesus is baptized
    Luke 3:21–22 · narrative · AD 28 – AD 29

    As Jesus prays after being baptized, heaven opens, the Spirit descends like a dove, and a voice declares Him God's beloved Son.

    Baptism and the Lord's SupperThe Holy Spirit
  6. The transfiguration
    Luke 9:28–36 · vision · AD 29 – AD 30

    On a mountain, Jesus' face changes and His clothes shine as Moses and Elijah speak with Him about His departure. A cloud covers them, and a voice says, 'This is My Son, whom I have chosen. Listen to Him!'

    GloryThe cross
  7. Jesus rejoices in the Father
    Luke 10:21–24 · prayer · AD 29 – AD 30

    Jesus rejoices in the Spirit and thanks the Father for revealing things to little children, and says only the Father and Son know each other. He tells the disciples they are blessed to see what many prophets and kings longed to see.

    Pride and humilityJoy and thanksgiving
  8. The Word, life, and light
    John 1:1–5 · poetry

    John opens by describing the Word as existing in the beginning, with God and as God, the agent of creation, and the source of life and light that darkness has not overcome.

    The incarnationCreationGlory
  9. Persecution and claim to equality
    John 5:16–18 · narrative · AD 28 – AD 29

    Because Jesus healed on the Sabbath and called God His own Father, the authorities seek to kill Him. Jesus says His Father is at work and so is He.

    Opposition and persecution
  10. The Son gives life and judges
    John 5:19–29 · teaching · AD 28 – AD 29

    Jesus says the Son does what He sees the Father doing, gives life and has been given authority to judge. Those who hear and believe pass from death to life, and all will hear His voice at the resurrection.

    ResurrectionFinal judgmentEternal life
  11. Before Abraham was, I am
    John 8:48–59 · dialogue · AD 29

    Opponents accuse Him of being a Samaritan with a demon. Jesus says those who keep His word will not see death, claims Abraham rejoiced to see His day, and says 'before Abraham was born, I am'. They pick up stones.

    Names of GodOpposition and persecution
  12. I and the Father are one
    John 10:22–30 · dialogue · AD 29 – AD 30

    At the Feast of Dedication in winter, the Jews ask Him to say plainly whether He is the Christ. He points to His works, says His sheep hear His voice and are secure, and declares 'I and the Father are one.'

    Eternal lifeThe Messiah
  13. Stones and Scripture
    John 10:31–39 · dialogue · AD 29 – AD 30

    The Jews pick up stones for blasphemy. Jesus points to His good works, quotes 'you are gods' from their Law, and argues that His works show the Father is in Him. They try to seize Him, but He escapes.

    Opposition and persecutionLaw and commandments
  14. Thomas asks the way; Jesus is the way
    John 14:5–7 · dialogue · AD 30 – AD 33

    Thomas says they do not know where Jesus is going. Jesus answers that he himself is the way, the truth and the life, and that knowing him means knowing the Father.

    Salvation
  15. Philip asks to see the Father
    John 14:8–14 · dialogue · AD 30 – AD 33

    Philip asks Jesus to show them the Father. Jesus replies that seeing him is seeing the Father, points to his words and works, and promises that believers will do great works and that prayer in his name will be answered.

    The incarnationFaith
  16. Love, obedience and the coming Spirit
    John 14:15–21 · teaching · AD 30 – AD 33

    Jesus links love for him with keeping his commands and promises the Father will send another Advocate, the Spirit of truth, to be with them forever. He says he will not leave them as orphans but will come to them.

    The Holy SpiritLoveObedience
  17. The true vine and its branches
    John 15:1–8 · teaching · AD 30 – AD 33

    Jesus calls himself the true vine, the Father the vinedresser and the disciples the branches. Branches must stay attached to bear fruit, and those that do not are removed and burned.

    DiscipleshipObedience
  18. Many gifts, one Spirit
    1 Corinthians 12:4–11 · teaching · AD 54 – AD 55

    Paul says there are varieties of gifts, ministries, and workings but the same Spirit, Lord, and God. Each person receives a manifestation of the Spirit for the common good, as the Spirit determines.

    Spiritual giftsThe Holy SpiritUnity
  19. From slaves to sons
    Galatians 4:1–7 · teaching · AD 48 – AD 55

    Paul compares the time before Christ to an heir under guardians. When the time was right, God sent his Son to redeem those under the law, so believers are adopted and God's Spirit cries 'Abba, Father' in their hearts.

    RedemptionFreedomThe incarnation
  20. Blessed with every spiritual blessing
    Ephesians 1:3–6 · praise · AD 60 – AD 62

    Paul praises God for blessing believers in Christ, choosing them before the world began, and adopting them as his children for the praise of his grace.

    Election and callingGlory
  21. Prayer for strength and love
    Ephesians 3:14–21 · prayer · AD 60 – AD 62

    Paul kneels before the Father and asks that they be strengthened by the Spirit, that Christ dwell in their hearts, and that they grasp the breadth of Christ's love. He ends with praise to God.

    PrayerLove
  22. Christ is supreme over all creation
    Colossians 1:15–20 · poetry · AD 60 – AD 62

    In an exalted passage, Paul describes the Son as the image of the invisible God, through whom all things were created and hold together. He is head of the church, first to rise from the dead, and through His cross God reconciles all things.

    CreationGloryThe incarnation
  23. God has spoken by his Son
    Hebrews 1:1–4 · teaching · AD 60 – AD 69

    The author says God spoke in many ways through the prophets but in these last days by his Son. The Son is heir of all things, creator, radiance of God's glory, and now sits at God's right hand, far above the angels.

    GloryAtonement and sacrificeCreation
  24. The Son is greater than the angels
    Hebrews 1:5–14 · argument · AD 60 – AD 69

    Through a chain of Old Testament quotations, the author shows the Son is God's Son, worshiped by angels and eternal on his throne. The angels, by contrast, are servants sent to help those who will inherit salvation.

    Angels and spiritual beingsKings and kingshipWorship and praise
  25. Greeting to scattered believers
    1 Peter 1:1–2 · letter · AD 62 – AD 65

    Peter, an apostle, greets God's chosen people living as exiles across several provinces of Asia Minor. He describes them as chosen by God, sanctified by the Spirit and sprinkled with Christ's blood.

    Election and callingThe Holy Spirit
  26. Remaining in God and in love
    1 John 4:13–16 · teaching · AD 85 – AD 95

    Believers know they remain in God because He has given His Spirit. The apostles testify that the Father sent the Son as Savior of the world, and whoever confesses Jesus as the Son of God has God abiding in them.

    The Holy SpiritLove
  27. The testimony about the Son
    1 John 5:6–12 · teaching · AD 85 – AD 95

    Jesus came by water and blood, and the Spirit testifies to this, so the Spirit, the water and the blood agree. God's testimony is greater than human testimony, and it is that God gave eternal life in His Son.

    The Holy SpiritThe incarnation
  28. Introduction and blessing
    Revelation 1:1–6 · letter · AD 68 – AD 96

    John introduces the book as a revelation of Jesus Christ given through an angel, pronounces a blessing on those who read and keep it, and greets the seven churches with grace and peace.

    Dreams, visions and revelationThe end times
