  7. Gideon and the fleece

    Judges 14:5–9 · narrative · 1075 BC – 1062 BC

    On the way to Timnah, a young lion attacks Samson and the Spirit of the LORD lets him tear it apart bare-handed. Later he finds bees and honey in the carcass, eats some, and shares it with his parents without telling them where it came from.
