    Exodus 34:1–9 · narrative · 1446 BC

    God tells Moses to cut two new tablets and climb Sinai alone. The LORD descends in a cloud and declares he is compassionate, gracious, slow to anger and forgiving, yet does not overlook guilt. Moses worships and asks him to go with them.

    Names of GodMercy and compassion
