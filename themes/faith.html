  1. Enoch walked with God
    Genesis 5:21–24 · genealogy

    The pattern breaks with Enoch, who walks with God for 300 years and does not die like the others, because God takes him.

    Death and mortality
  2. Noah's righteousness and the coming end
    Genesis 6:9–13 · narrative

    Noah is described as righteous and blameless, walking with God, with three sons. The earth is corrupt and violent, and God tells Noah he will destroy it.

    Justification and righteousness
  3. God calls Abram with a promise
    Genesis 12:1–3 · speech · 2092 BC – 2090 BC

    The Lord tells Abram to leave his country and family for a land he will show him. He promises to make Abram a great nation, bless him, and bless all families of the earth through him.

  4. Abram refuses the king of Sodom's reward
    Genesis 14:21–24 · dialogue · 2085 BC – 2082 BC

    The king of Sodom offers Abram the goods in exchange for the people. Abram, swearing by God Most High, refuses to take anything so no one can say he made Abram rich.

    Wealth and poverty
  5. God's promise of offspring and Abram's faith
    Genesis 15:1–6 · dialogue · 2083 BC – 2081 BC

    The Lord tells Abram not to fear and that he is his shield. Abram, still childless, questions this, and God promises a son and offspring as many as the stars. Abram believes, and it is counted as righteousness.

    Genesis 16:1–6 · narrative · 2081 BC – 2080 BC

    Childless Sarai gives her Egyptian servant Hagar to Abram. When Hagar conceives and looks down on Sarai, Sarai blames Abram, and then treats Hagar so harshly she flees.

  7. Sarah will bear Isaac
    Genesis 17:15–22 · dialogue · 2068 BC – 2066 BC

    God renames Sarai as Sarah and promises she will bear a son, Isaac. Abraham laughs and pleads for Ishmael, and God agrees to bless Ishmael but says the covenant will be with Isaac.

    Exodus 14:10–14 · dialogue · 1446 BC
