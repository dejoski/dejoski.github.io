18 verses · 333 words · ~2 min read · ~2 min listen

Paulʼs Hardships and Godʼs Grace

1As Godʼs fellow workers, then, we urge you not to receive Godʼs grace in vain. 2For He says:

“In the time of favor I heard you,
and in the day of salvation I helped you.”

Behold, now is the time of favor; now is the day of salvation!

3We put no obstacle in anyoneʼs way, so that no one can discredit our ministry.

4Rather, as servants of God we commend ourselves in every way: in great endurance; in troubles, hardships, and calamities; 5in beatings, imprisonments, and riots; in labor, sleepless nights, and hunger; 6in purity, knowledge, patience, and kindness; in the Holy Spirit and in sincere love; 7in truthful speech and in the power of God; with the weapons of righteousness in the right hand and in the left; 8through glory and dishonor, slander and praise; viewed as imposters, yet genuine; 9as unknown, yet well-known; dying, and yet we live on; punished, yet not killed; 10sorrowful, yet always rejoicing; poor, yet making many rich; having nothing, and yet possessing everything.

11We have spoken freely to you, Corinthians. Our hearts are open wide. 12It is not our affection, but yours, that is restrained. 13As a fair exchange-I speack as to myy children-open wide your hearts also.

Do Not Be Unequally Yoked

14Do not be unequally yoked with unbelievers. For what partnership can righteousness have with wickedness? Or what fellowship does light have with darkness? 15What harmony is there between Christ and Belial? Or what does a believer have in common with an unbeliever? 16What agreement can exist between the temple of God and idols? For we are the temple of the living God. As God has said:

“I will dwell with them
and walk among them,
and I will be their God,
and they will be My people.”
17“Therefore come out from among them
and be separate, says the Lord.
Touch no unclean thing,
and I will receive you.”

18And:

“I will be a Father to you,
and you will be My sons and daughters,
says the Lord Almighty.”

Prophecy & fulfillmentLinked by the New Testament

I will dwell among them and walk among them

God promises at Sinai to set his dwelling among Israel and walk among them. Paul applies this promise to the church at Corinth, calling them the temple of the living God, and strings it together with other promises, including the one to David's son in 2 Samuel 7:14.

When: Leviticus (Sinai era); 2 Corinthians, c. AD 55–56.

Original passageLeviticus 26:11–12Ezekiel 37:27
Where the New Testament applies it2 Corinthians 6:16–18

Prophecy & fulfillmentLinked by the New Testament

Now is the day of salvation

Paul quotes Isaiah's promise that God answers his servant 'in the time of favor' and 'in the day of salvation', and says 'now' is that day. He applies the servant's words to the present moment of the gospel's proclamation.

When: Isaiah, c. 700–540 BC; 2 Corinthians, c. AD 55–56.

Original passageIsaiah 49:8
Where the New Testament applies it2 Corinthians 6:1–2
